What the slice represents

The current scenario, Chapter 0: GP referral to treatment, models one fictional GP practice, referral office, outpatient specialist and scanner. Its cohort and queues are synthetic; named residents are fictional.

Examples of its modelling choices:

  • A cohort of 600; 120 arrivals per week, with 10% urgent and 40% referred, are game-balance settings for playable queues.
  • One diagnostic modality and a 60% chance of diagnostics after an outpatient consult simplify a larger care pathway.
  • Referral completeness changing from 70% to 92% is a hypothetical experiment, not a measured improvement.
  • The 12-week waiting threshold is a simulation simplification, not a verified statement of a current standard.

These are not measurements of a real hospital. The complete parameter values and their provenance tags are in the scenario file.

What the results do not claim

  • No mortality, lives-saved or clinical-effectiveness claims.
  • No calibration to a Dutch hospital or region.
  • No claim that AI improves real healthcare outcomes.
  • No real patient data or connection to a healthcare record system.

Comparisons describe this model under the selected scenario and seed. They are not clinical, national or causal evidence.

Evidence status

A SOURCED tag means a source is recorded; it does not by itself mean the source has been verified. Evidence-register rows E-01–E-04 are currently unverified. Their figures must not be presented as established facts until the primary sources and exact wording are checked.

All outputs should be read as synthetic. Structural queueing lessons may be informative, but their magnitudes are specific to this model and its assumptions.
